CHOOSE YOUR MATERIAL

Four high-quality filaments to suit every application and budget.

PLA
Polylactic Acid
The most popular FDM material. Biodegradable, easy to print, great surface finish. Ideal for prototypes, display models, and general use.
from £0.05 / cm³ material
PETG
Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol
Tougher than PLA with better heat and chemical resistance. Food-safe grades available. Great for functional parts and enclosures.
from £0.07 / cm³ material
ABS
Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene
Impact-resistant with high temperature tolerance. Post-processable with acetone for smooth finishes. Excellent for mechanical parts.
from £0.06 / cm³ material
TPU
Thermoplastic Polyurethane
Flexible and rubber-like. Excellent for gaskets, grips, phone cases, and any part requiring elasticity or vibration damping.
from £0.10 / cm³ material
