ABOUT 3DPRINTFACTORY

WHO WE ARE

3DPrintFactory is a UK-based on-demand FDM 3D printing service. We make professional-quality prints accessible to everyone — from hobbyists and makers to product designers and small businesses.

Every print is produced on calibrated FDM machines with verified filament stock, inspected by a human before it leaves our workshop, and dispatched with tracked delivery.

OUR PROMISE

Transparent pricing. Every quote shows exactly what you’re paying for — materials, supports, setup, and delivery. No hidden fees.

Privacy first. Your STL file is parsed entirely in your browser for instant quoting. It is only uploaded when you place an order.

Quality guaranteed. If a print doesn’t meet our standards, we reprint it at no charge.

TECHNOLOGY

We use Fused Deposition Modelling (FDM) — the most versatile and cost-effective 3D printing technology for functional parts and prototypes. Typical layer height: 0.1–0.3 mm. Build volume: up to 300×300×400 mm.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

  • We currently accept .STL (stereolithography) files — the industry-standard format for FDM printing. Binary and ASCII STL are both supported. Maximum file size is 50 MB. If your model is larger, please decimate it in your CAD software before uploading.
  • The price is calculated from the actual geometry of your model:

    1. Effective volume: We compute the model’s solid volume using the divergence theorem, then apply your chosen infill density — because the interior is not printed solid.

    2. Material cost: Effective volume × material price per cm³.

    3. Support surcharge: We detect overhanging faces (downward-facing normals beyond 45°) and add a proportional surcharge for the support material and extra print time.

    4. Setup fee: A flat £2.50 per order covers machine prep and quality inspection.

    5. Delivery: Calculated by weight (print + 300 g packaging). Royal Mail Tracked 48 for orders under 2 kg, Evri Standard for heavier orders.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: March 2026

What data we collect

When you place an order we collect: your name, email address, postal address, and phone number. We do not store payment card details — payment is processed by our payment provider and card data is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

STL files

Your STL file is parsed client-side in your browser for the purpose of generating an instant quote. The file is only transmitted to our servers when you submit an order. Uploaded files are retained for order fulfilment and deleted after 90 days.

Cookies and tracking

We use localStorage and sessionStorage to persist your cart and order information within your browser session. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact privacy@3dprintfactory.co.uk with any requests.

Data sharing

We share your shipping address with our courier partners (Royal Mail, Evri) solely for order delivery. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

RETURNS POLICY

Quality guarantee

Every order is inspected before dispatch. If your print is defective (layer delamination, significant dimensional error, surface defects beyond normal FDM characteristics), contact us within 7 days of delivery with:

  • Your order reference number
  • Clear photographs of the defect
  • A description of the issue

We will offer a free reprint or full refund at our discretion.

Non-returnable items

Custom 3D prints are made to order and cannot be returned simply because you changed your mind, or because of inherent FDM characteristics (visible layer lines, slight surface texture). Please review material properties and request a sample print if unsure.

Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled free of charge within 2 hours of placement, provided printing has not yet commenced. Email orders@3dprintfactory.co.uk immediately with your order reference.
